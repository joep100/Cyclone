How we work this out

On our Shared service, your delivery travels with others along the same run. We call the saving Carbon Share. We take the vehicle’s emissions for each kilometre and divide them between the deliveries on board. We base this on a typical run carrying 5 deliveries at a time. Each one then carries a fifth of what a dedicated run would produce, which is where the 80% figure comes from. Some runs carry more and some carry fewer.
