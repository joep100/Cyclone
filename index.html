Tracking widget, against the real system

Nothing on this page is faked. The widget talks to book.cyclone.ie/api/track, so put in a real job number and you will get the real status.

Two ways to open it

The black button in the bottom right, which the script adds on its own.

Or any link you like: track a delivery.

What to expect

A job booked and waiting says Booked. Once a rider accepts it, On the way. Then Collected, and Delivered at the end. A number that does not exist says nothing was found.

A link can open it straight onto a job, which is what would go in the text message: add ?track=11097627 to the address.
